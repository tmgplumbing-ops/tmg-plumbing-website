faqs:
- q: Do you offer maintenance contracts?
  a: Yes. We can agree an ongoing maintenance plan for your business, or simply quote for one-off maintenance and upgrades as they come up.
- q: Can you work while the building is in use?
  a: Yes. Most of the work is done while the system is running, through a bypass into our filtration unit, and we plan around your timetable so occupants are disturbed as little as possible.
- q: Do you need to drain the heating system?
  a: Usually not. Our equipment filters the sludge and debris out while the water circulates, and in most cases no cleaning chemicals are needed. We'll tell you in advance if a heavily blocked section needs a different approach.
- q: Do you work outside the South East?
  a: Yes. We carry out commercial power flushing, demineralisation and water treatment anywhere in Ireland.
- q: How often should a commercial heating system be flushed?
  a: It depends on the system and its water quality. We recommend testing the water every six months; the results show when cleaning or treatment is needed, rather than relying on guesswork.
