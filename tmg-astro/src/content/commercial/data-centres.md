about:
  heading: Why water quality matters for cooling
  paragraphs:
  - Chilled-water loops serving CRAH units, air handlers and heat exchangers rely on clean water to move heat efficiently. Corrosion products, biofilm and debris build up over time, clogging strainers, fouling plate heat exchangers and making control valves less reliable, all on equipment you can't easily take out of service.
  - We clean the water while the system keeps running, using side-stream filtration through a bypass, so there is no need to shut down cooling. Any isolation is planned with your team in advance and fitted around your redundancy, so a backup is always available.
signsHeading: Signs your chilled-water loop needs attention
signs:
- Strainers clogging or needing cleaning more often
- Plate heat exchangers losing performance
- Control valves sticking or hunting
- Dirty, discoloured or cloudy water samples
- Water results drifting out of the limits set by your equipment manufacturers
- Higher pump energy or falling flow rates
stepsHeading: How we work on live cooling systems
steps:
- title: Survey and risk review
  body: We review the schematics, redundancy, isolation points and access with your facilities team, and agree method statements, risk assessments and change requests before any work starts.
- title: Baseline water sampling
  body: Samples are analysed for pH, conductivity, hardness, corrosion products and suspended solids, giving a clear picture of the loop's condition.
- title: Side-stream filtration
  body: Water is taken through a bypass into our mobile filtration unit while the system keeps running, removing debris and magnetite without interrupting cooling.
- title: Targeted cleaning
  body: Where a strainer, heat exchanger or section needs more attention, we plan it around your maintenance windows and redundancy so cooling capacity is protected.
- title: Treated top-up water
  body: Make-up water can be demineralised before it enters the loop, so topping up doesn't bring fresh minerals into the system.
- title: Monitoring and reporting
  body: We re-test the water after the work and can set up a regular sampling schedule, with a short report each time showing results and trends.

faqs:
- q: Can you clean the system without shutting down cooling?
  a: Yes, in most cases. Side-stream filtration cleans the water through a bypass while the loop keeps running. Anything that needs isolating is planned with your team around your redundancy.
- q: Do you work with glycol systems?
  a: Many cooling loops contain glycol. Tell us what's in your system and we'll confirm the right approach before any work is planned.
- q: How often should chilled-water quality be tested?
  a: It depends on the system and its history. As a starting point we suggest testing every six months, then adjusting the interval based on the results.
- q: Do you provide reports for our maintenance records?
  a: Yes. Each visit comes with a short report covering what was done, the water-quality results and any recommendations.
- q: Do you work outside the South East?
  a: Yes. We carry out commercial and industrial system cleaning and water treatment anywhere in Ireland.
