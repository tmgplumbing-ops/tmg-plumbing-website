about:
  heading: What a GMP site needs from a contractor
  paragraphs:
  - Heating and chilled-water loops feed air handling units, clean-room HVAC and process equipment. Sludge, magnetite and scale in those loops reduce heat transfer, foul heat exchangers and strainers, and make temperature control harder to hold. On a regulated site, the contractor working on them matters as much as the result.
  - We work to your site's rules. Work is scoped in advance, method statements and risk assessments are submitted for approval, and nothing changes on a system without going through your change control. When the job is finished you receive a written record of what was done, with water-quality results before and after.
signsHeading: Signs your heating or cooling loops need attention
signs:
- Air handling units or process equipment struggling to hold temperature
- Heat exchangers, strainers or control valves fouling more often
- Dirty, discoloured or cloudy system water
- Water results drifting out of the limits set by your equipment manufacturers
- Pumps, valves or plate heat exchangers failing or needing more maintenance
- Higher energy use for the same load
stepsHeading: How we plan and carry out the work
steps:
- title: Survey and scoping
  body: We review system schematics, volumes, isolation points and access with your engineering team, and agree the scope, working hours and any shutdown window.
- title: Documentation for approval
  body: We prepare a method statement, risk assessment and work plan for your review, and complete your site induction and permit-to-work process before starting.
- title: Baseline water sampling
  body: Samples are taken from agreed points and analysed for pH, conductivity, hardness, corrosion products and suspended solids, so there is a clear starting point.
- title: Filtration and cleaning
  body: Where possible, water is cleaned through a bypass into our mobile filtration unit while the loop keeps running. Sections that need isolating are cleaned during your planned shutdown.
- title: Demineralisation and refill
  body: The water is demineralised and the system topped up with treated water, with reference to VDI 2035 and the water-quality requirements of your equipment manufacturers.
- title: Results and handover
  body: We sample the water again and hand over a report covering the method, the results before and after, and recommendations for ongoing monitoring.

- heading: Shutdown planning
  paragraphs:
  - Many pharmaceutical sites work to fixed annual or periodic shutdowns. We plan the work well in advance so that the parts that need isolating are ready to go when the window opens, and as much as possible is done beforehand while the system is live.
  points:
  - Pre-shutdown survey and water sampling

  - Isolation and cleaning of agreed sections during the shutdown
  - Post-shutdown sampling to confirm the result

faqs:
- q: Do you work within our change control and validation procedures?
  a: Yes. We provide the method statements, risk assessments and records your quality and engineering teams need to assess the work, and nothing is changed on a system until it has been approved through your procedures.
- q: Can the work be done without a full shutdown?
  a: Often, much of it can. Side-stream filtration cleans the water while the loop keeps running. Where a section has to be isolated, we plan it into your shutdown window.
- q: Do you work on purified water or product-contact systems?
  a: No. We work on closed heating and cooling loops, such as LTHW and chilled-water circuits, and not on purified water, water for injection or other product-contact systems.
- q: Do you use cleaning chemicals?
  a: In most cases no chemicals are needed, because the cleaning is done by filtration and magnetic separation. If a heavily fouled section needs a treatment product, we agree it with you in advance and supply the safety data sheet.
- q: Do you work outside the South East?
  a: Yes. We carry out commercial and industrial system cleaning and water treatment anywhere in Ireland.
