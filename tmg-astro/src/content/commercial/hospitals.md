about:
  heading: What a hospital heating system needs
  paragraphs:
  - Patient rooms, theatres, critical care units and corridors all need steady, consistent heat, and a failure in any one area affects patient comfort and the delivery of care. Stable conditions also matter for medical equipment that relies on a controlled environment.
  - Over time, sludge, magnetite and limescale build up inside radiators, underfloor heating and pipework. Heat stops reaching parts of the building, pumps and boilers work harder, and the risk of breakdown rises. We clear that build-up and treat the water so the system stays clean, working in sections so clinical areas are not disrupted.
signsHeading: Signs your building needs a power flush
signs:
- Cold spots, or some rooms warm while others stay cold
- Radiators cold at the bottom but warm at the top
- Banging, rumbling or gurgling from boilers and pipework
- The building takes longer to warm up in the morning
- Rising energy bills, especially through the winter
- Recurring boiler, pump or valve breakdowns
stepsHeading: How we power flush a commercial heating system
steps:
- title: Site survey
  body: We look over the heating plant, pumps, pipework, radiators, fan coils and underfloor circuits to see where sludge is gathering, and plan the work around how your building operates.
- title: Water analysis
  body: We test a sample of the system water for pH and corrosion, and use a magnet to show how much magnetic debris is circulating.
- title: Bypass filtration
  body: While the system runs, water is taken through a bypass into our specialist filtration unit, removing the sludge and debris that block pumps, valves and pipework. In most cases there is no need to drain down.
- title: Power flushing
  body: Each section of the system is flushed at high velocity but low pressure, while magnetic filters capture the metal particles that cause future corrosion.
- title: Demineralisation
  body: The water is passed through a demineralisation unit, removing the minerals that cause scale and leaving it balanced and non-corrosive.
- title: Refill and protect
  body: The system is topped up with treated water to VDI 2035, and a Heating Water Regulator can be fitted to keep it protected long term.
- title: Final check
  body: We test the water again and check heat distribution, flow rates, boiler performance and radiator recovery times.

faqs:
- q: Can you work around clinical areas?
  a: Yes. We plan the work with your estates team and flush the system in sections, so wards and theatres keep their heating while we work.
- q: Can you work while the building is in use?
  a: Yes. Most of the work is done while the system is running, through a bypass into our filtration unit, and we plan around your timetable so occupants are disturbed as little as possible.
- q: Do you need to drain the heating system?
  a: Usually not. Our equipment filters the sludge and debris out while the water circulates, and in most cases no cleaning chemicals are needed. We'll tell you in advance if a heavily blocked section needs a different approach.
- q: Do you work outside the South East?
  a: Yes. We carry out commercial power flushing, demineralisation and water treatment anywhere in Ireland.
- q: How often should a commercial heating system be flushed?
  a: It depends on the system and its water quality. We recommend testing the water every six months; the results show when cleaning or treatment is needed, rather than relying on guesswork.
