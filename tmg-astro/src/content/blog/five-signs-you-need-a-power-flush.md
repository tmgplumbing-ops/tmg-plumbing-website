A heating bill that's crept up without any change in how the house is used is usually the first sign something's wrong inside the system. Sludge — a mix of rust and debris that builds up in pipework and radiators over time — restricts water flow and forces the boiler to work harder for the same result.

Here are the five most common signs it's time for a power flush:

**1. Radiators cold at the bottom, warm at the top.** Sludge settles at the bottom of the radiator, blocking circulation to that section.

**2. Some rooms never warm up.** If certain radiators barely heat at all while others work fine, the blockage is likely more advanced in those parts of the system.

**3. Banging or gurgling from the boiler.** Trapped air and debris moving through partially blocked pipework often causes this noise.

**4. Water runs black or discoloured when a radiator is bled.** This is a direct sign of sludge in the system.

**5. The boiler cuts out or short-cycles more than it used to.** A boiler working against restricted flow can trip more frequently.

If two or more of these sound familiar, a power flush is worth considering. It typically takes a single day, restores even heat across the whole system, and reduces the strain on your boiler going forward.
