Every year, once the heating goes on for the first time in autumn, the same pattern repeats: households discover half their radiators are cold, and call-outs for power flushing spike all at once.

Booking before that rush has two advantages. First, availability — appointments are easier to arrange in September than in the first cold week of November. Second, timing — a power flush is more straightforward to carry out while the heating system isn't in daily use.

**Signs worth checking now, before the cold weather arrives:**
- Cold spots on radiators
- Boiler noise that wasn't there last winter
- A heating bill that was higher than expected last season

Getting a power flush done in autumn means going into winter with a system that's already running at full efficiency, rather than discovering problems on the first cold night.
