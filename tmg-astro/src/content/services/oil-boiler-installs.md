faqs:
- q: How often should an oil boiler be serviced?
  a: Manufacturers recommend an annual service. It keeps the boiler running safely and efficiently and helps protect its lifespan and warranty.
- q: How long does an oil boiler installation take?
  a: It depends on the boiler, the location and whether the tank or controls are being changed too. We'll confirm how long the work will take when we quote, so you can plan around it.
- q: What is a bunded oil tank?
  a: A bunded tank is a tank within a tank. If the inner tank leaks, the outer bund contains the oil, giving far better protection and longer warranties than an old single-skin tank.
- q: Should I flush my heating system when fitting a new boiler?
  a: Yes. Sludge from an old system can damage a new boiler, and many manufacturers require the system to be cleaned. We can power flush and demineralise the system as part of the installation.
