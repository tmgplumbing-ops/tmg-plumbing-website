about:
  heading: How underfloor heating works
  paragraphs:
  - Underfloor heating circulates warm water through a network of pipes laid beneath the floor. Heat rises gently and evenly from the whole floor area, giving a comfortable temperature across the room rather than hot spots near a radiator.
  - Because it runs at lower water temperatures than radiators, underfloor heating is very efficient, particularly in well-insulated homes and when paired with a heat pump. It can also be zoned, so you can keep some rooms warmer than others depending on how they are used.
signsHeading: Signs your underfloor heating needs attention
signs:
- Cold patches or areas of the floor that never warm up
- Rooms that take much longer to heat than they used to
- Pressure dropping or needing to be topped up
- Noisy pump or air in the manifold
- Rising energy bills with no change in use
- The system hasn't been serviced in a few years

- heading: Underfloor heating not working properly? Start here
  paragraphs:
  - Over time, sludge and debris settle in the pipes under your floor. The heat can no longer move around the system properly, so you notice cold spots and the heating working harder than it should. A power flush and a proper service clear out the build-up and get everything flowing again, so the heat circulates the way it is meant to.

faqs:
- q: Does underfloor heating need to be serviced?
  a: Yes. Like any heating system it needs regular maintenance. A service checks the manifold, pumps, controls and pressure, and keeps heat circulating efficiently.
- q: Can you power flush underfloor heating?
  a: Yes. We flush each underfloor circuit individually with a filtration machine, removing the sludge that causes cold patches and slow heat-up.
- q: Why is part of my floor cold?
  a: The most common causes are sludge restricting the flow in a circuit, trapped air, a faulty actuator or pump, or circuits that are out of balance. We check each of these during a service.
- q: Does underfloor heating work with a heat pump?
  a: Very well. Underfloor heating runs at lower water temperatures than radiators, which is exactly where a heat pump is most efficient.
- q: Is underfloor heating expensive to run?
  a: In a well-insulated home it is one of the most efficient ways to heat, because it runs at lower temperatures while keeping the house comfortable. Keeping the system clean and balanced keeps running costs down.
